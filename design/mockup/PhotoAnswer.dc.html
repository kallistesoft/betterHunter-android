Correct

Mule deer

Cerf mulet · Odocoileus hemionus
How to recognise it

Very large ears and a white rump patch with a small, thin, black-tipped tail. The buck's antlers branch into repeated forks (Y-shaped).

Where it lives

Western Canada: BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, southwestern Manitoba and southern Yukon.

Don't confuse it White-tailed deer: broad tail, white underneath, raised like a flag when it runs.
